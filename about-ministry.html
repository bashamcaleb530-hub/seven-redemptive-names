My Ministry Foundation

Seven Names One Redeemer exists to proclaim Jesus Christ, teach God’s Word, and help people grow in their understanding of who God is and what He has provided through redemption.

This statement explains the beliefs, calling, and ministry commitments that guide my work. Scripture is my foundation, and I continue studying God's Word as I grow in understanding.

1. My Calling and Ministry Purpose

I believe God has called me to preach the Gospel of Jesus Christ, teach His Word, help people grow spiritually, and reveal God’s love and character through Jesus Christ.

I began recognizing my calling to ministry at nineteen years old. Although I have experienced insecurity and have had to grow in confidence, I believe God has called me to serve Him and help others.

One important part of my calling is reaching people who are lost, spiritually struggling, or have been hurt through their experiences with churches or religious people. I want people to understand who God truly is, how much He loves them, and what Jesus Christ has accomplished through redemption.

My ministry is centered on Jesus Christ, the one Redeemer through whom God’s provision is revealed.

Scripture references: Mark 16:15; Matthew 28:18–20; Luke 19:10; John 14:9; Ephesians 4:11–13.

2. God the Father, Jesus Christ, and the Holy Spirit

I believe in one God in three distinct Persons: God the Father, Jesus Christ the Son, and the Holy Spirit. Each is fully God, and they work together in perfect unity.

God the Father is our heavenly Father, whom we can approach, speak to, and have a personal relationship with. Jesus Christ is the Son of God and the living Word made flesh. Through Him, we come to know the Father. We approach the Father in Jesus’ name because of what Jesus accomplished through His death and resurrection.

The Holy Spirit is the Spirit of God who dwells within believers. He is our Comforter, Helper, Teacher, and Guide. He strengthens us, brings God’s Word to our remembrance, and helps us understand spiritual truth.

Scripture references: John 1:1–14; John 14:9, 16–26; John 16:13; Matthew 28:19.

3. Salvation, Redemption, and Righteousness

I believe God created mankind for relationship with Him. Through Adam’s disobedience, sin and death entered the world. God is righteous and just, and sin could not simply be ignored. But because God is love, He provided redemption through His Son, Jesus Christ.

Jesus died for our sins, shed His blood for the forgiveness of sins, and rose again from the dead. Salvation is received by grace through faith in Jesus Christ, not earned through good works or religious performance.

We must genuinely believe in our hearts that Jesus is Lord, that He died for our sins, and that God raised Him from the dead. We must receive Jesus Christ as our Lord and Savior. Merely saying we believe without sincerely believing in our hearts is not the same as receiving salvation.

I believe our righteousness before God comes through Jesus Christ, not through our own ability to live perfectly. The righteousness we have through Him is the foundation of our relationship with the Father.

Scripture references: Genesis 2–3; Romans 3:21–26; Romans 5:12–19; Romans 10:9–13; Ephesians 2:8–9; 2 Corinthians 5:21.

4. The Seven Redemptive Names of God

I believe the Seven Redemptive Names reveal important aspects of God’s character and provision, which we come to understand through Jesus Christ.

These names are not a replacement for Jesus. My ministry is not centered on the names themselves, but on Jesus Christ, our complete Redeemer.

  • Jehovah-TsidkenuThe LORD Our RighteousnessJeremiah 23:6
  • Jehovah-ShalomThe LORD Our PeaceJudges 6:24

  • Jehovah-JirehThe LORD Will ProvideGenesis 22:14

  • Jehovah-ShammahThe LORD Is ThereEzekiel 48:35

Scripture references: Jeremiah 23:6; Judges 6:24; Exodus 15:26; Genesis 22:14; Psalm 23:1; Ezekiel 48:35; Exodus 17:15.

5. God’s Word, Faith, and Spiritual Growth

I believe God’s Word must have first place in the believer’s life. The Holy Spirit is essential to understanding spiritual truth. When I study, I pray for understanding, read Scripture, meditate on it, and return to it repeatedly as my understanding grows. I do not believe in forcing a particular feeling or experience.

I believe God’s Word is like a seed planted in the heart. Spiritual growth develops as we receive the Word, continue meditating on it, speak in agreement with it, and put it into practice. Like a seed growing in the ground, growth may take time. We should not give up simply because we do not immediately see the results.

Faith is not just repeating words to convince ourselves that we believe. Faith involves genuinely believing God’s Word, trusting what He has said, and responding to it.

I believe our minds need continual renewal through God’s Word so that our thinking becomes increasingly governed by spiritual truth rather than relying only on what our physical senses tell us.

Scripture references: Mark 4:3–32; Joshua 1:8; Proverbs 4:20–23; Romans 10:17; Romans 12:2; 1 Corinthians 2:10–14; James 1:22.

6. Healing, God’s Love, and His Correction

I believe healing, including physical healing, is part of God’s redemptive provision through Jesus Christ. I understand Isaiah 53:4–5, Matthew 8:16–17, and 1 Peter 2:24 as important Scriptures concerning healing.

I believe God loves us and desires that we know His character rather than live in fear of Him. God’s love also includes correction. His correction may be uncomfortable, but its purpose is to help us grow and mature.

Even when we have studied God’s love for many years, I believe there is still more for us to understand. I want to remain teachable and open to correction through God’s Word and the Holy Spirit.

Scripture references: Isaiah 53:4–5; Matthew 8:16–17; 1 Peter 2:24; John 3:16; Romans 8:38–39; Hebrews 12:6–11; 1 John 4:18.

7. Prayer and the Holy Spirit

I believe prayer is personal communication with God and an important part of developing a relationship with our heavenly Father. Prayer is not only speaking to God. I believe we should also listen for His guidance.

The Holy Spirit helps us pray, understand God’s Word, and discern His direction. I believe He can guide us through an inward witness and, at times, through spiritual gifts and experiences that must be examined in light of Scripture.

I believe the baptism in the Holy Spirit is distinct from being born again, and that speaking in tongues is its initial evidence. I believe tongues can be used for personal prayer and spiritual edification without a public interpretation. When someone gives a message in tongues publicly to the congregation, interpretation is needed for the church to be edified.

I also believe that when believers pray in heavenly tongues, they are speaking mysteries to God that Satan cannot understand. I am continuing to study that particular understanding further.

Scripture references: John 14:26; John 16:13; Acts 1:8; Acts 2:1–4; Romans 8:26–27; 1 Corinthians 14.

8. Water Baptism and Communion

I believe salvation is received through faith in Jesus Christ. Water baptism is an outward expression of what Jesus has already accomplished within the believer; water baptism itself is not a requirement for salvation.

I believe communion is a remembrance of Jesus Christ and His redemptive work. The bread represents His body, which was given for us and wounded for our healing. The wine or grape juice represents His blood, which was shed for the forgiveness of sins.

I believe communion should be taken with reverence. Believers should examine themselves, sincerely repent where needed, and partake in a worthy manner. Partaking in an unworthy manner is not the same as saying we must become personally perfect or earn the right to receive communion through our works.

Scripture references: Acts 8:36–38; Romans 10:9–13; Luke 22:19–20; Matthew 26:26–28; 1 Corinthians 11:23–32.

9. Evangelism and Discipleship

I believe every believer has a responsibility to share the Gospel of Jesus Christ. Jesus commanded His followers to go into all the world and preach the Gospel to every creature. He did not limit that responsibility to pastors, evangelists, prophets, or other people who hold ministry offices.

Every believer is called to share the Gospel, but that does not mean every believer holds the office of pastor, evangelist, prophet, or another ministry leader.

I believe many people throughout the world have never heard the Gospel. Some of them might receive Jesus Christ if someone would share it with them. This is one reason evangelism is so important. I do not believe a person is automatically saved because they have never heard the Gospel. I believe they still need to receive Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior.

I also believe new believers need teaching, encouragement, and a strong foundation in God’s Word so they can grow and mature spiritually.

Scripture references: Mark 16:15; Matthew 28:18–20; Acts 8:4; John 14:6; Acts 4:12; Romans 10:14–17; Ephesians 4:11–13.

10. Pastoral Ministry and Caring for People

I believe a pastor is a shepherd serving under Jesus Christ, the Chief Shepherd. Pastors should guide, protect, encourage, teach, and equip God’s people.

I want to help people who have been hurt, including those who have experienced church hurt, discouragement, fear, or personal struggles. Correction should be given with love, gentleness, and appropriate accountability.

I believe ministers must practice what they teach and remain accountable for their actions. When I make a mistake, I believe in acknowledging it, seeking appropriate counsel, apologizing when necessary, and correcting what needs to be changed.

I believe pastoral care must include responsible boundaries and safeguards, especially in private meetings and whenever children or vulnerable people are involved. Care for people must never become an excuse for secrecy, manipulation, or misconduct.

Scripture references: John 21:15–17; 1 Peter 5:2–4; Galatians 6:1; James 3:1; 1 Timothy 3:1–7.

11. Ministry Leadership and Financial Accountability

I believe ministry leadership should operate with integrity, honesty, accountability, and appropriate oversight. Important ministry decisions should involve responsible leadership and board oversight rather than depending entirely on one person.

I believe ministry finances should involve at least two trustworthy people with separate financial responsibilities and appropriate board oversight. One person should not have unchecked control over receiving, recording, approving, and spending ministry funds.

If theft or financial misconduct is suspected, I believe it should be taken seriously, addressed promptly, independently investigated, and reported to the proper authorities when appropriate. Restitution and accountability should be pursued where applicable.

I believe a person may eventually be spiritually restored following genuine repentance, but that does not mean the person automatically returns to a position of financial trust.

Scripture references: Proverbs 11:14; 2 Corinthians 8:20–21; 1 Peter 5:2–3; 1 Timothy 3:1–13.

12. Preparing and Teaching God’s Word

I believe ministers should continually read, study, and meditate on God’s Word so they are prepared to minister. I believe in establishing the main point clearly, teaching Scriptures in their context, and developing the explanation through related Scriptures, illustrations, and practical application.

Preparation is important, but our ultimate dependence must remain upon the Holy Spirit.

I have experienced times when I was asked to preach with little time to prepare because another pastor became sick or was injured. I have also experienced times when I had time to prepare but did not know what message to teach, or when I believed the Holy Spirit was directing me to change a message shortly before preaching.

These experiences have helped me learn to depend on the Holy Spirit rather than only on my own preparation. I want to keep growing in my ability to explain spiritual truths clearly so people can understand them.

Scripture references: Joshua 1:8; John 14:26; 2 Corinthians 3:5; 2 Timothy 2:15; 2 Timothy 4:2.

13. The Return of Jesus Christ

I believe Jesus Christ will return for His people. Believers who have died in Christ will be raised first, and believers who remain alive will be caught up together with them to meet the Lord. I believe we will receive glorified, immortal bodies and be with Jesus forever.

Concerning the timing of the rapture, I currently lean toward it happening during the earlier portion of the tribulation, although I believe it could happen before the tribulation begins. I am continuing to study this subject.

Scripture references: John 14:1–3; 1 Thessalonians 4:13–18; 1 Corinthians 15:51–53.

14. Eternal Life, Heaven, Hell, and Judgment

I believe eternal life comes through Jesus Christ alone. A person must genuinely believe in and receive Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior to be saved.

I believe heaven and hell are eternal. Those who receive Jesus Christ have eternal life with Him, while those who do not receive salvation face eternal judgment. I believe this is true even for those who have never heard the Gospel, which makes sharing it especially urgent.

God does not desire anyone to perish, but desires people to come to repentance and receive salvation through Jesus Christ.

I believe people can receive salvation during the tribulation. I am continuing to study the precise sequence of end-time events and judgments. I do not treat unverified accounts of people returning from hell as a settled teaching about second chances after death.

Scripture references: John 3:16–18; John 14:6; Acts 4:12; Romans 10:9–17; Matthew 25:46; Hebrews 9:27; 2 Peter 3:9; Revelation 7:9–14; Revelation 20:11–15.

My Overall Ministry Purpose

My desire is to preach Jesus Christ, reach people with the Gospel, help believers establish a strong foundation in God’s Word, and teach them how to grow spiritually.

I want people to know God’s love, understand His character, recognize what Jesus accomplished through redemption, and learn to apply God’s Word to their lives.

My purpose is to glorify God, help people come into a relationship with Jesus Christ, and equip them to grow into maturity through His Word and the Holy Spirit.

This is a statement of the ministry’s beliefs and convictions. Areas identified as still being studied are not presented as settled conclusions.
