Your Foundation for Standing in Truth

We were never meant to face the battles of life in our own strength. God has given us His Word, His promises, His strength, and everything we need to stand firm in faith.

The power God has placed within every born-again believer is real, but if we do not know what Jesus accomplished for us, what belongs to us through redemption, or how to use the truth of God's Word, we will struggle to walk in the freedom and victory He has provided.

Foundation Truth:
We cannot fight spiritual battles with our own strength or abilities. We need God's strength, God's truth, and the spiritual weapons He has already provided.

This software is designed to help you understand those truths, learn how to stand on them, and apply them to real situations in life.
