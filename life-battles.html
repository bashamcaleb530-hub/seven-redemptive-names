Important support and safety information

This center provides biblical teaching, Scripture, prayer, reflection, and practical next steps. It does not diagnose a medical or mental-health condition and does not replace qualified medical, mental-health, addiction, pastoral, or emergency care.

If you may hurt yourself or someone else, if you are in immediate danger, or if an overdose or other medical emergency may be occurring, contact local emergency services now. In the United States, you can call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. For substance use, alcohol, or prescribed medicines, do not use this page as a detox plan; some forms of withdrawal can be medically dangerous. Seek qualified medical guidance.
